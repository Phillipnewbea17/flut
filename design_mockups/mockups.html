Design 02

Pure White Minimal

Calm, airy and modern. White space does the work, and green is used only where it matters — the brand mark, the current step and the main button. Feels like a premium banking app.

  • Maximum contrast and readability
  • Step-by-step progress tracker for the application
  • Actions as simple list rows — one tap, no guessing
  • SOS kept visible but calm, not alarming
